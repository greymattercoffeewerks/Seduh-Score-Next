# Organiser UX S5: wording table (spec item U6)

_Drafted 2026-11-11 from `origin/dev` at `0be5593` (S1 to S4 merged). Wording only: no hint keys, behaviour, layout or CSS change._

**Vocabulary used throughout:** match · round 1 / round 2 · the cut (and "the cut line") · knockout · scheduled · scoring ·
confirmed · level (never "tied") · tie-break · seed · "lock the top 8". Never "trio". British spelling. Short sentences:
what happened, then what to do. No "database", "RPC", "votes", "cut-off", "preliminary" (the organiser sees round 1 and
round 2) in organiser text.

**Three groups.** A: organiser-only strings, applied directly. B: strings the committee reads (the printed sign-off sheet),
**held for the owner's approval before they change**. C: kept as they are, or listed as follow-ups because they are audience
facing or live in a shared core module.
