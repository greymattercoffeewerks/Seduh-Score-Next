BTC live surfaces: planned screens

Static mockups, not built. Each frame below is the real design system: the real token files, the real venue-display band and frame, and Cup Taster's real projector and phone stylesheets, plus a small mockup-only stylesheet for the BTC-specific parts (design/mockups/btc-mock.css). Each projector frame is a live 16:9 page, so it scales exactly as it will on the room's screen. Demo data throughout.

Reading the notes. Reused means an existing class or module, unedited. New means BTC-only work. The rounded first drafts from the conversation are the comparison; these are the faithful version.

Projector (the venue screen)

1. Standings

Shows
Place, team, wins, points. Eight rows a page, so 24 teams is three pages with the change ring.
When
The idle loop between matches.
Data
The existing standings (the same ranking the organiser sees).
Built from
Reused table classes, paging, ring, band. New column set.

2. Up next

Shows
The next match, both teams, their seeds, the judges; what follows in the footer.
When
The idle loop, and straight after a result moment.
Data
Derived from match order. No new organiser action (open decision 1).
Built from
New two-sided card on the shared screen frame.

3. Result recorded

Shows
Points per side, the breakdown (tokens, +5 for winning, +2 fastest), who goes through. The winner wears the heavy outline and says so in words.
When
A short moment (about 6 s) after a match is confirmed, then back to the loop.
Built from
Reused the moment player. New the screen.

3b. Rank impact (added after checking the shared work)

Shows
A headline and the top places, each with a word: Up 1, Down 1, Holds, New. Colour only adds emphasis.
When
A moment (about 8 s) after a PRELIMINARY match is confirmed, because that is when the table moves. Not used in the knockout.
Built from
Reused entirely: the rank-movement logic and rows, the rise-in reveal, the moment player. This frame uses the shared stylesheet unedited.

4. A level match, decided

Shows
Equal points, and the organiser's decision as a gold pill. Never hidden.
Open
Whether the reason text ("sudden-death cup") appears here or only "Decided by tie-break" (open decision 2). The reason is typed by the organiser.
Built from
Same as screen 3. Gold stays a fill, per the design rules.

5. Bracket

Shows
Quarterfinals, semifinals, and final with third place. Winners bold, played slots outlined, undecided slots say so.
When
The idle loop once the bracket exists.
Data
The same bracket data and winner rule as the organiser screen and the podium.
Built from
New. The biggest new screen.

6. Champion and podium

Shows
Champion, final score, runner-up and third place.
When
Once the final and third place are decided. Stays up.
Built from
Reused Cup Taster's champion classes unedited; only the wording is BTC's.

Phone (scrolls, 360px first)
